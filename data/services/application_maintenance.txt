APP IN SNAP - APPLICATION MAINTENANCE

App in Snap provides application maintenance and technical support services after application deployment.

Maintenance services include:

- Application bug fixing
- Technical issue resolution
- Performance improvements
- Application updates
- Compatibility improvements
- Database-related maintenance
- API and integration support
- Post-deployment technical assistance

Customers can contact App in Snap when they need maintenance, troubleshooting, or technical assistance for an application developed or supported by App in Snap.
