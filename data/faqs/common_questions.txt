APP IN SNAP - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How can I register a complaint?

A: You can register a complaint through the App in Snap support system. Provide information about your issue and a unique complaint number will be generated.

Q: How can I check my complaint?

A: You can check your complaint using your complaint number.

Q: What is a complaint number?

A: A complaint number is a unique identifier assigned to your complaint after successful registration.

Q: Can I have multiple complaints?

A: Yes. A customer can register multiple complaints. Each complaint receives its own complaint number.

Q: What information should I provide when registering a complaint?

A: Provide a clear description of the problem and any relevant information that can help the support team understand the issue.
