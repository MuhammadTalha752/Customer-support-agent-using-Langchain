APP IN SNAP - COMPLAINT POLICY

Customers can register complaints regarding services, applications, technical problems, or other service-related issues.

Complaint Registration:

1. The customer must provide sufficient information about the problem.
2. A complaint is assigned a unique complaint number after registration.
3. The customer can use the complaint number to check the complaint status.
4. Customers should keep their complaint number for future reference.

Complaint Status:

A complaint can have different statuses during its lifecycle.

Registered:
The complaint has been successfully received.

In Progress:
The support team is currently investigating or working on the complaint.

Resolved:
The reported issue has been resolved.

Closed:
The complaint has been completed and closed.

If additional information is required, the support team may contact the customer.
